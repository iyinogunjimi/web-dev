About

I'm a 200-level cybersecurity student at Bowen University, working toward a career as a SOC analyst and security researcher. My coursework spans data protection, computer architecture, system analysis, and Java programming, and I spend my time outside class turning that theory into hands-on practice.

I run a home lab where I deploy tools like Wazuh SIEM and Splunk to practice log analysis and incident triage, and use Wireshark to study network traffic at the packet level. I'm also building a networking foundation through the Cisco CCNA, and I use Bash scripting to automate parts of my workflow.

On the development side, I've built full-stack projects including an e-commerce platform for a footwear brand with Paystack payment integration, and I use tools like Figma, Adobe XD, and After Effects for design work. I like the overlap between building systems and knowing how to secure them — it means I can think like both a developer and an attacker.

I'm currently preparing for an upcoming internship and sharpening my skills in SPL and KQL query writing, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, and vulnerability assessment. My goal is to grow into a well-rounded analyst who understands both the offensive and defensive sides of security.
